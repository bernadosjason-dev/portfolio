Athlos: pickleball for a whole city, in one app.

Players in Cagayan de Oro were finding games in group chats, booking courts by text and paying by screenshot, and tracking tournaments on paper. Athlos puts all of it in one place — ratings, open play, court booking, tournaments and a portal for the venues — and automates the admin that used to eat an organizer’s day.

What it does

Players

An Athlos Rating from 2.000 to 8.000 for singles, doubles and mixed. Only refereed games move it, so the number means something. Form, clutch games and partner chemistry on top.

Open play

Find a game tonight by day, level and price. Check in, join a fair queue, and get called to your court by push.

Courts

Every venue in the city with directions. Pick a free slot, pay by GCash, the venue confirms. Refunds follow written rules, not arguments.

Tournaments

Set up in five steps. Groups + knockout, round robin or elimination. The schedule builds itself: every match gets a time, a court and a referee, and the knockout runs as the evening main event.

Referees

Certified referees with their own desk and ID. Assigned automatically, rebalanced when a match moves, called to court on their phone.

Venues

A separate portal to approve payments, add walk-ins and run open play. App bookings, walk-ins and open play share one calendar, so nothing double-books.

03

What’s automated

  • Scheduling. Draw the bracket and every match is timed, placed on a court and given a referee. Move a match and the referee load rebalances.
  • Reminders and call-to-court. Scheduled jobs remind players before their match and buzz their phone when their court is ready.
  • Booking holds and refunds. Unpaid holds expire on their own; refunds are due by rule when a venue declines or cancels.
  • Ratings. Verified results update ratings and leaderboards with no one entering numbers by hand.
  • Monitoring. An agent checks errors, failed jobs and support messages every hour, and drafts a fix branch when it finds a real bug.
04

How it’s built

Next.js on Vercel, Supabase for Postgres, auth, storage and scheduled jobs. Business rules live in the database behind row-level security, so the phone app, the venue desk and the organizer console all enforce the same rules. Web push for alerts. Designed, built and run solo.
