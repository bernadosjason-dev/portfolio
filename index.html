A‑01

Profile

I build SaaS for construction — and I’m one of the few people building it who has actually done the work. Civil engineering graduate, two years on site, and since 2024 running the sales side of a modular manufacturing business.

Somewhere between the takeoff sheets and the quote deadlines I started building the tools I wished existed — a kitchen estimator, then a lead pipeline, then a full project system. Most construction software is designed by people who have never priced a job or chased a supplier. Mine is designed against real jobs, with real money on the line, and the difference shows up in the details nobody else thinks to handle.
